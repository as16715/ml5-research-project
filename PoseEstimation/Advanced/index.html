Bunny Puppet Theater

Step in front of your camera and become the bunny. Pose tracking pins fluffy ears to your head, a pink twitchy nose with whiskers and blush to your face, and big cartoon paws to your hands. Raise your wrists above your shoulders and your bunny extends its claws — lower them and the claws sheath back into soft pink toe pads.

How to perform

  • Stand back so your head and shoulders are clearly in frame.
  • Look straight at the camera so the ears anchor cleanly to your head.
  • Lift your wrists above your shoulders to extend the claws.
  • Lower your hands to sheath them back into soft toe pads.
  • Watch the nose twitch — the bunny is alive even when you're still.
